# Project Proposal Feedback

The project is clearly described and well motivated, and the main goal of measuring how MBTA bus performance varies across Boston and who is most affected is easy to follow. The goals would be stronger with more measurable criteria. For example, define what counts as "consistently abnormal service" (such as a delay threshold or a z-score cutoff), choose the metrics and baseline for the delay prediction model (such as RMSE or MAE compared against a simple historical-average baseline), and specify how you will calculate "hours lost" per rider. The data sources are identified but not linked, so please name and link the specific MBTA performance datasets and the census tables you plan to use (for example, ACS median income and race by tract). Also describe how you will join bus stops or routes to neighborhoods, since that step is important to the equity analysis. The timeline is reasonable and the buffer week is a good idea, but it does not assign tasks to team members, and model training starts late with no time set aside for model evaluation or writing the final report. Finally, please cite the source for the $11.5 billion figure and proofread for small typos (such as "to us MBTA bus data" and "broken up my routes").
